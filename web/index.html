    How a score is made

    Four marks, then adjustments

    Open source is other people’s projects, up to 35. Your own repos alone stay at 10 or under. Self projects, up to 30, reward live products and mark down tutorial apps and missing links. Production, up to 25, is shipped work. Technical skills, up to 10, count when they show up in that work.

    Bonus covers a portfolio, LinkedIn, a technical blog, Google Summer of Code, GirlScript Summer of Code, and founder or early-startup roles. Deductions cover tutorial-only projects, generic names, and broken or missing links.
